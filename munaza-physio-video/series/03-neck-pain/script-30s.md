## Why it's built this way

- **Question hook in the first second:** "Where's your head right now?" gets viewers checking their own posture, which stops the scroll. The first frame is already the scene and captions.
- **Cut every ~2 s** for the first 11 s, with a whip and a whoosh on each cut, and three quick "is this you?" questions.
- **Pattern break:** the music drops out and a record scratch plays on "one quick fix", then the reveal switches to light and a brighter beat.
- **Visible before/after payoff:** the setup changes in one continuous shot (same chair and desk), so the result is clear even with the sound off.
- **Captions carry it all** with the sound off: word-by-word, the current word in brand red.
- **No claims:** no cure, no guarantee, no stats, no testimonials. Movement is "at your comfort". The plan is "a plan that fits your real day".

## Character rules kept
- **Ayesha:** deep-red hijab covering hair, neck and chest; loose black abaya.
- **Profile views:** the hood wraps under her chin into the drape, so the neck never shows, even when she tilts or lies down.
- **Dr. Munaza:** a mask over nose and mouth, and no mouth is drawn. Navy hijab, white coat over navy, white closed shoes.
- **Sofa scene:** both stay seated for the whole assessment.
