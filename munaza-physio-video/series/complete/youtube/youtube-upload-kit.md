| **SEO** (YouTube + Google search) | Rank for searched phrases | Keyword-first title, description first line, tags, chapters, captions file |

## 11. Claims check (for YouTube's medical content policy)

The title, description and pinned comment use only "assessment", "guided exercise", "may help" and "step by step". They never say cure, guaranteed, 100% or a recovery time. Keep it that way in replies to comments too: YouTube's medical misinformation policy and Pakistan's advertising rules both apply to health claims.
