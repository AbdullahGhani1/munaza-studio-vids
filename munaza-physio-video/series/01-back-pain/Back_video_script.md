## Format (category, flavor, why)

- **Category:** UGC-style musical short (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- **Flavor:** self-deprecating desi humor, then warm reassurance
- **Why:** back pain is universal and "my back makes noise" is instantly shareable. UGC styling (comment-reply sticker, jump cuts, handheld feel) reads as native content, not an ad

## Topic and angle

- **Topic:** everyday back pain in women: sitting, lifting children, housework
- **Angle:** back pain as a noisy, dramatic character in your life. The belief "just rest" is challenged by assessed, guided movement

## Storytelling structure

MAESTRO: funny hook, relatable pain, rising stakes, silent question, twist, expert resolution, CTA and an open loop into the next episode.

## Hook analysis

- **Line:** "My back makes more noise than my family WhatsApp group."
- **Why it works:** specific, local, funny, under 3 seconds. The comment-reply sticker makes it feel like a real reply to a viewer. It opens a loop: why does my back do that, and what can I do?

## Visual layout (9:16)

- **Top 15%:** platform UI safe zone; only the comment sticker in the hook
- **Middle 60%:** main visual (cards, doctor image, kinetic words)
- **Lower 25%:** word-by-word captions, max 2 lines, above platform UI
- **UGC texture:** seeded handheld drift, punch-in zooms on beats, jump cuts every 1.5-3 s in the verse
- **Palette:** red `#E7212B`, dark red `#C80710`, black `#101010`, white `#FFFFFF`, soft `#FFF3F3`

## Dopamine addiction loop

1. **Open loop** at 0:00: a funny problem with no answer yet
2. **Micro-rewards** every 1.5-3 s: a cut, a kinetic word, a card snap, a beat hit
3. **Recognition:** "that's literally me" moments (laundry, sofa, heating pad, weddings)
4. **Tension spike:** silence at 0:26 makes viewers lean in
5. **Payoff:** twist plus expert answer
6. **New open loop:** "Next: Neck Pain" pulls viewers into episode 2

## Stakes

Back pain starts taking away carrying your child, getting dressed, family weddings and your mornings.

## Big question

"Is this just my life now?" (0:26), in full silence.

## Headfake

- **Expected answer:** rest more
- **Twist:** "What if my back is asking me to move?" Assessed, guided movement is the reveal, kept responsible with "No magic cure"

## Art of contrast

- Bouncy, funny music against real frustration
- Loud, chaotic verse against a silent black-screen question
- "Rest more" against "Move"
- Shaky handheld cuts against calm, steady framing of Dr. Munaza

## Rehook

- Chorus "Move like you mean it" at 0:32 brings attention back after the silence
- Outro callback to the WhatsApp hook closes the loop
- Series rehook: "Next: Neck Pain" end card

## Audio plan

- **Primary:** generate a new 60 s track from `Back_lyrics.md` (vocals clear, music low)
- **Fallback:** `Move_Like_You_Mean_It.mp3` as a bed at 10-15% volume under a clear voiceover of the lyrics. Mute it at 0:26-0:32 and keep it at about -24 dB under the phone number
